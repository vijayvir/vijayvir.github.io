
GPS TUNES — PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: September 6, 2026
Developer: AntFire Labs
Contact: virvijay37@gmail.com

AntFire Labs ("we", "us", "our") built GPS Tunes ("the App") with your
privacy in mind. This Privacy Policy explains what data the App accesses,
how it is used, and the choices you have.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * Your LOCATION is used ONLY on your device to detect geofence entry
    and exit. It is not sent to us.
  * Your VOICE RECORDINGS stay ONLY on your device. We never receive them.
  * Your APPLE MUSIC playback is handled by Apple's frameworks; we do not
    receive your listening history.
  * Non-Pro users see ads served by GOOGLE ADMOB, which processes
    advertising identifiers according to your consent and system settings.
  * SUBSCRIPTION STATUS is handled by Apple's StoreKit; we do not store
    payment details.
  * GEOFENCE DATA is stored locally on your device via SwiftData. We do
    not sync it to a server.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

GOOGLE ADMOB (non-Pro users only)
We use Google AdMob to serve advertising. AdMob may collect information
such as your device's advertising identifier (IDFA — only if you granted
App Tracking Transparency permission), IP address, general location
(city-level), and interaction with ads.

Where required (EU / EEA / UK / Switzerland), we present the Google User
Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form the first time the App loads ads.
You may reset your ad-consent choice from Settings on your device.

Google privacy policy:      https://policies.google.com/privacy
Manage ad personalization:  https://support.google.com/ads/answer/2662922

APPLE SERVICES
The App uses Apple frameworks including MusicKit, CoreLocation, StoreKit,
and User Notifications. These are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy:
https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

STOREKIT 2 (subscriptions)
Purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We only receive an entitlement
flag telling us whether your Pro subscription is active. We do not receive
your Apple ID, name, or payment method.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. WHAT WE DO NOT DO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * We do not operate a user account system — no email/password is
    required to use the App.
  * We do not sell or rent your personal data.
  * We do not upload your location, recordings, listening history, or
    geofence data to any server.
  * We do not use analytics SDKs that transmit personal data to us.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
GPS Tunes is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect
data from children under 13. If you believe your child has provided data to
us, please contact us at virvijay37@gmail.com.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. YOUR RIGHTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct,
delete, or restrict the processing of your personal data. Because we do
not maintain a server-side account for you, most of these rights can be
exercised directly on your device:

  * Delete all data — uninstall the App.
  * Revoke location, microphone, or notification permissions —
    Settings → Privacy.
  * Reset your ad-consent choice — Settings → GPS Tunes (if available in
    your region).
  * Manage or cancel your subscription — Settings → Apple ID →
    Subscriptions.

For any additional requests, email virvijay37@gmail.com.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. DATA RETENTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Because we do not receive your data on our servers, retention is entirely
under your control. Deleting the App removes all local data. Backups you
have made to iCloud or iTunes may retain a copy — consult Apple's
documentation.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. INTERNATIONAL USERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
If you use the App outside your country of residence, be aware that the
App runs entirely on your device; no cross-border transfers of data by us
occur. Third-party services (Google, Apple) may transfer data
internationally per their own privacy policies.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
9. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated"
date reflects the most recent revision. Material changes will be
communicated in-app where reasonable.
